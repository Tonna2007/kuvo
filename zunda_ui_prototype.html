Why these choices

Onboarding is phone-first, not email — matches how Nigerians actually sign up for messaging apps, and skips a step WhatsApp itself doesn't have.

Low data mode stays visible on the chats screen, not buried in settings — it's a stated selling point, so it should be seen, not just configurable.

Gist replaces the generic "Status" label — it's what people already call catching up on updates, and it keeps stories and campus posts in one screen so it costs one tap, not two.
